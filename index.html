This archive brings together cases of people whose deaths have been linked to police violence, detention or confinement in Spain.

The cases differ substantially. Some occurred during arrests or while people were in custody; others are connected with police interventions, immigration detention centres (CIEs), deportations or actions involving officers off duty.

Some cases resulted in judicial convictions. In others, the circumstances remain disputed, under investigation, or incompletely documented.
